Calibration test

How well does your confidence match what you actually remember? About twelve minutes. Read at your normal pace; there is no reward for speed.

Before you start

You will guess answers to four questions before reading. Wrong guesses are expected and useful — they make the later reading stick. Then you read, rate yourself twice, do a two-minute unrelated task, and take the test.
